LEVEL 1

What is Mahjong?

Mahjong is a game for 4 players. You play with small blocks called tiles. Each tile has a picture on it.

Everyone tries to collect tiles that go together, like collecting sets of cards. The first person to finish a full hand of 14 tiles shouts "Mahjong!" and wins the round.

FUN FACTPeople say the clacking tiles sound like chirping sparrows. "Mahjong" can mean "sparrow"!

LEVEL 2

Meet the tiles

There are 144 tiles. Don't worry! There are only a few families to learn.

3 number families. They go from 1 to 9, and there are 4 copies of every tile.

Dots

Count the circles. Five circles means number 5.

Bamboo

Count the sticks. Number 1 is a little bird, not a stick!

Characters

These use Chinese writing. The red sign at the bottom is the same on all of them. Look at the top sign for the number. Tip: 1, 2 and 3 have 1, 2 and 3 lines!

Picture families. No numbers here. There are 4 copies of each one too.

Winds

The 4 directions on a map.

Dragons

Red, Green and White. White is just a blue frame.

Flowers (bonus!)

Only 1 of each. They never go in your hand. They are little gifts. See Level 6.

Let's add them up:

  • Dots, Bamboo, Characters: 9 × 4 copies × 3 families = 108
  • Winds: 4 × 4 = 16
  • Dragons: 3 × 4 = 12
  • Flowers and seasons: 8

108 + 16 + 12 + 8 = 144 tiles!

LEVEL 3

How do you win?

You need 14 tiles made of 4 groups and 1 pair.

group+group+group+group+pair

There are 3 kinds of groups. Each one has a funny name.

Chow

3 tiles in a row, from the same family. Like counting 4, 5, 6!

Pung

3 tiles exactly the same. Like triplets!

Kong

4 tiles exactly the same. A super pung! Still counts as 1 group.

Pair

2 tiles the same. Some people call the pair "the eyes".

Careful! These are NOT groups:

  • A chow uses one family only. No mixing bamboo and dots.
  • A chow has no gaps. 2, 4, 6 skips numbers.
  • Winds and dragons can't make a row. Only pungs, kongs and pairs.

LEVEL 5

Get the table ready

  1. Mix the tiles. Turn them all face down and swirl them around. This is called "washing the tiles". It sounds great!
  2. Build a wall. Each player builds a wall 18 tiles long and 2 tiles tall. Push the 4 walls together into a big square.
  3. Pick who goes first. One player is East. East rolls the dice. A grown-up can help open the wall in the right spot.
  4. Take your tiles. Everyone takes 13 tiles. East takes one extra, so East has 14.
  5. Stand your tiles up so only you can see them. Keep them secret!

Pro tip: sort your tiles by family. It makes groups much easier to find.

LEVEL 6

Pick one, throw one

East goes first. East already has 14 tiles, so East just throws one away. After that, every turn has 2 parts:

1. Pick up

Take 1 new tile from the wall. Now you have 14.

2. Throw away

Put 1 tile you don't want face up in the middle and say its name. Back to 13.

Then it's the turn of the player on your right. Around and around!

Got a flower? Lucky you!

Put it face up next to you. Then take a new tile from the back end of the wall. Flowers give bonus stars later.

LEVEL 7

"I want that tile!"

If someone throws away a tile you need, you can grab it! But you must shout fast.

Pung!

You have 2 matching tiles and someone throws the 3rd. Shout "Pung!" and take it. It can come from anyone.

Kong!

You have 3 matching tiles and someone throws the 4th. Shout "Kong!" Then take one more tile from the back of the wall.

Chow!

You need one tile to finish a row. Shout "Chow!" only if it came from the player who went right before you (on your left).

Show your new group face up on the table, then throw one tile away. The next turn goes to the player after you. Sometimes this skips people!

Two people want the same tile?

  1. "Mahjong!" always wins.
  2. Then "Pung!" or "Kong!"
  3. "Chow!" comes last.
LEVEL 8

Shout "Mahjong!"

When your 14 tiles make 4 groups and 1 pair, you win!

You can win with a tile from the wall, or with a tile someone threw away. Lay all your tiles face up so everyone can check.

Chow + Pung + Chow + Pung + Pair = 14 tiles. Mahjong!

Nobody won? If the wall runs out of tiles, the round is a tie. Mix the tiles and play again.

LEVEL 9

Counting stars

Grown-ups use lots of scoring rules. To start, try this easy star score. Only the winner gets stars.

If your winning hand has…Stars
You won! (any winning hand)★ 1
You picked the winning tile from the wall yourself+1
Each pung or kong of dragons+1
Each flower you collected+1
All pungs, no chows+3
All tiles from one family (dots only, for example)+5

Play 4 rounds. Most stars at the end is the champion!

LEVEL 10

Be a great player

  • Say the name of every tile you throw away, like "3 bamboo!"
  • Keep your tiles secret until you win.
  • Watch what others throw. If everyone throws dots, maybe no one is collecting them.
  • Keep the middle tiles. 4, 5 and 6 can join more rows than 1 or 9.
  • Take your time, but not too long. Others are waiting.
  • Win nicely, lose nicely. Say "good game" to everyone!
